Overview

How it fits your stack

The IMPS Switch is the rail beneath every instant fund transfer your customers make — 24x7, 365 days a year, including bank holidays and after banking hours. It handles Person-to-Person (P2P) and Person-to-Account (P2A) transfers using mobile number + MMID or account number + IFSC, and routes each request through NPCI with the validation, risk checks and settlement logic already built in.

Banks and PPIs plug into a single Mobee integration and get multi-bank reach, automated reconciliation, and a dashboard that surfaces every failed, pending and successful transaction in real time — so support teams aren't chasing settlement files at 2am.

How It Works

From request to settlement

STEP 01
Request initiation

Customer initiates a transfer from your app; Mobee captures beneficiary and amount details.

STEP 02
Validation & risk check

Limits, KYC status and fraud rules are checked before the request leaves your system.

STEP 03
NPCI IMPS routing

The switch routes the validated request to the beneficiary bank via the NPCI IMPS network.

STEP 04
Settlement & notification

Funds move in real time; both parties receive instant confirmation and the ledger updates.

Key Features

Everything the switch handles for you

24x7x365 availability

No batch cut-offs, no maintenance windows that block customer transfers, including weekends and holidays.

P2P & P2A transfers

Support account+IFSC and mobile+MMID based transfers in a single unified flow.

Multi-bank routing

Route to any NPCI-connected bank without separate integrations per destination.

Auto reconciliation

T+0 reconciliation against NPCI settlement files, with exception reports auto-flagged.

Sub-second response

Optimized routing paths keep average response times under two seconds at peak load.

Built-in risk checks

Velocity, limit and blacklist checks run before every request reaches NPCI.
