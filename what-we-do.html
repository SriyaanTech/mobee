What We Do

One connected rail for every payment mode

Twelve modular, RBI/NPCI-aligned products — deploy independently or as one full-stack switching and payments platform.

Switching

The rails behind instant, interoperable payments across banks and networks.

IMPS Switch

24x7, 365-day interbank fund transfer switching with sub-second response.
UPI Switch

PSP-grade UPI switching — Collect, QR, Autopay and multi-bank routing.
TPAP Switch

Launch your own UPI-enabled app as a licensed Third Party Application Provider.
BBPS Switch

Bharat BillPay Operating Unit switch for bill aggregation across categories.
Assisted Banking

Last-mile banking access through Business Correspondent networks.

AEPS

Aadhaar-enabled biometric micro-banking at the doorstep.
DMT

Domestic money transfer for migrant and unbanked customers.
MATM

Micro-ATM banking software on a handheld device.
Cards & Digital Value

Issuance and distribution products that put value in customers' hands.

Recharge & Gift Vouchers

Mobile, DTH and digital gift voucher distribution APIs.
Prepaid Cards

End-to-end prepaid card issuance and program management.
Collections & Risk

Acceptance, collection and protection layers for every transaction.

Bill Payment

Utility & recurring collections beyond the BBPS network.
Payment Gateway

Omni-channel payment acceptance for merchants.
FRM

Real-time fraud & risk monitoring across every channel.
One Platform

Why deploy through one stack instead of twelve vendors

Every Mobee product shares the same compliance framework, the same reconciliation logic, and the same MobeeShield risk engine underneath it. That means one vendor relationship, one integration pattern, and one place to look when something needs an answer — instead of stitching together a dozen point solutions.
