What We Do

Twelve switches, one connected rail

Every payment mode Indian consumers use every day, engineered as modular, RBI/NPCI-aligned switches you can deploy independently or as one full-stack platform.

IMPS Switch

24x7 instant interbank fund transfer switching.
UPI Switch

PSP-grade UPI switching, Collect, QR & Autopay.
TPAP Switch

Launch your own UPI handle as a licensed TPAP.
BBPS Switch

Bharat BillPay Operating Unit for bill aggregation.
AEPS

Aadhaar-enabled biometric micro-banking at the doorstep.
DMT

Domestic money transfer for the assisted-banking network.
MATM

Micro-ATM banking on a handheld device.
Recharge & Gift Vouchers

Prepaid Cards

End-to-end prepaid card issuance & program management.
Bill Payment

Payment Gateway

Omni-channel payment acceptance for merchants.
