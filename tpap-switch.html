Overview

How it fits your stack

Becoming a UPI Third Party Application Provider (TPAP) usually means months of sponsor-bank negotiation and NPCI certification work before a single transaction flows. Mobee's TPAP Switch compresses that timeline: we bring pre-integrated sponsor bank relationships, NPCI-certified switch infrastructure, and an SDK your app team can drop straight into your product.

You keep full ownership of your brand, UX and customer relationship; Mobee handles VPA issuance, handle management, multi-bank fallback and the compliance plumbing that NPCI requires of every TPAP.

How It Works

From request to settlement

STEP 01
Registration

Mobee supports your NPCI TPAP registration and documentation.

STEP 02
Sponsor bank tie-up

We connect your app to pre-integrated sponsor banks for issuing and acquiring.

STEP 03
SDK integration

Your app team integrates the Mobee UPI SDK for Pay, Collect and QR flows.

STEP 04
Go live & scale

Launch under your own handle with multi-bank capacity from day one.
