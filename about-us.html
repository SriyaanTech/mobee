Who We Are · About Us

Making digital awesome since 2012

For 14+ years, Mobee Technologies has engineered the switching and orchestration infrastructure that lets banks, NBFCs and fintechs move money instantly, safely and at national scale.

Our Story

A full-stack fintech platform, built the hard way

Mobee Technologies began as a technology consultancy and grew into a CMMI Level 3, ISO-certified fintech infrastructure company. Today we operate the switching rails behind IMPS, UPI, TPAP, AEPS, BBPS and mobile banking journeys used by banks, cooperative institutions, NBFCs and fintech brands across India and the Middle East.

Our engineering teams design every layer of the stack in-house — from the front-end journeys customers touch, to the switch cores that talk to NPCI, to the fraud engine that watches every transaction in real time. That end-to-end ownership is what lets us move fast without compromising on regulatory rigor.

Infrastructure

Built by engineers, optimized for scale

Mobee provides a secure, scalable and reliable IT infrastructure designed to support modern business growth. We operate from development facilities spanning 5,000+ sq. ft., with delivery centers across Pune, Nepal, Dubai, Kenya and South Africa, and a team of 100+ full-time engineers across diverse technologies.

Our stack runs on AWS and Google Cloud with mature DevOps practices, continuous integration and delivery pipelines, and secure version-controlled repositories — giving clients a foundation that's both startup-fast and enterprise-safe.
